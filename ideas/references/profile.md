# A portable discovery profile

Use this shape for a requested handoff or saved profile. Omit empty sections, keep it compact, and retain enough source context to distinguish user statements from your interpretations. This is working context, not a personality assessment.

```markdown
# Discovery profile
Updated: YYYY-MM-DD

## Interests and direction
- Current interests, with a recent example:
- Near-term hopes and the user's timeframe:
- Long-term aspirations:
- Useful constraints and things they want to avoid:

## Knowledge and access
- Experience they described, with a concrete example:
- Concepts they recognize but have not used:
- People, communities, materials, or settings they can learn from:

## Open threads
- Observations, friction, surprises, or questions in the user's words:
- Tentative connections suggested by the guide, clearly labeled:

## Ideas and experiences
- Idea and origin: user-authored / guide-suggested / jointly developed
- Why it interests the user:
- Open assumption or question:
- Next experience and what it could reveal:
- What happened, if tried, and how that changed the idea:

## Resume here
- The thread the user wants to return to:
- The next useful question or experience:
```

On resuming, check the update date and refresh the relevant thread. Explicit user corrections take precedence over earlier summaries. Retain unresolved alternatives when useful; do not merge conflicting statements into a confident conclusion.

The skill can use a profile provided in the conversation without saving it. If the user asks for a saved file, use the authorized destination and ordinary filesystem permissions. Include only the information needed for the discovery work; do not copy a raw transcript or unnecessary personal details.
